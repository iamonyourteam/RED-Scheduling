The scheduling engine evaluates every available technician slot for each incoming call and picks the best fit using a weighted scoring system.

Scoring formula: Earlier days are preferred, then fuller cells (pack before opening new days), then geographic proximity. Skill mismatch and drive time violations are hard disqualifiers.
1
Skill Filter
Any tech whose skill level is below the job's requirement is immediately excluded. A New tech cannot take a Specialist call.
2
Capacity Check
The algorithm checks if the job + its drive time fits within the tech's remaining day capacity. Default workday is 9 hours. Configurable in Settings.
3
Drive Time Check
If placing the job would create any single transit leg over 30 minutes, that slot is skipped. Drive time is calculated using real Haversine distances × a 1.35 road factor at 28 mph.
4
Best Score Wins
Remaining valid slots are ranked. The lowest score (earliest day, fuller cell, closest address) wins. Jobs within a cell are then re-sorted largest→smallest.

Each technician's day is divided into three arrival windows. When a job is placed, it is automatically stamped with its window based on how many hours of work precede it. Customers are told their window — not an exact time — reducing scheduling pressure.

How windowing works: The algorithm accumulates job hours for each tech. When the running total crosses 3h (11 AM) or 6h (2 PM), jobs placed after that point receive the next window label. Divider lines appear in the calendar cell at each boundary.

About 5% of incoming calls are flagged ASAP — urgent jobs where the customer needs the earliest possible appointment. ASAP jobs behave differently from standard confirmed jobs in several key ways.

Visual cues: ASAP jobs have an amber ASAP badge instead of a lock icon, and a dashed left border to distinguish them at a glance.
1
Always Draggable
Standard confirmed jobs are locked. ASAP jobs remain draggable at all times so dispatchers can quickly move them if a better slot opens up.
2
Remove & Replace
Click any scheduled job → the detail modal shows ASAP jobs from later days that could fill this slot earlier. Click Remove & Replace to execute the swap in one action.
3
Eligibility Rules
Only ASAP jobs scheduled on a later day are suggested — moving a job backwards (to an earlier day) is the only valid ASAP optimization. The tech's skill level must also meet the job requirement.

The moment the algorithm places a job, it is considered confirmed — the customer has been given a time window and is expecting their technician. Accidental moves could break that promise.

Lock icon 🔒 means this appointment has been communicated to the customer. The card becomes non-draggable and shows a muted padlock badge in the top-right corner.

Every service call has a minimum skill requirement. Technicians hold a certification level and can only be assigned jobs at or below that level. A higher-skilled tech can always take a simpler job — not the reverse.

Expert Level 4
Can take all job types. Required for 6-hour complex jobs. Typically Jason Neff. ~3% of incoming calls.
Pro Level 3
Advanced calls and 4-hour jobs. Can take Specialist and New calls. ~0% default (adjustable in Settings).
Specialist Level 2
The most common tech tier. Handles the majority of calls (~57%). Can take New-level calls too.
New Level 1
Entry-level calls only (~40% of calls). Cannot take 6-hour jobs under any circumstances. Great for high-volume simple tasks.
Skill mismatch warning: If you manually drag a job onto a tech who lacks the required skill, a warning modal appears asking you to confirm or cancel. The algorithm never makes this mistake automatically.

The calendar supports full drag-and-drop for manual dispatch adjustments. Jobs in the queue, overflow panel, and ASAP calendar cards can all be repositioned.

1
Dragging from the Queue
Unscheduled chips in the incoming queue can be dragged directly onto any calendar cell. The algorithm will check skill and capacity — mismatches trigger a warning.
2
Dragging from Overflow
Jobs the algorithm couldn't place land in overflow. Drag them onto the calendar at any time. If the cell is over capacity you'll see a confirmation warning before placing.
3
Moving ASAP Cards
ASAP-flagged cards already on the calendar remain draggable. Drag them to any valid cell — confirmed status transfers to the new location automatically.
Confirmed cards (non-ASAP) show a lock icon and cannot be dragged. To move one, click it → Remove Job → then drag the overflow chip to the new location.

Click ⚙ in the dispatch controls to open the Settings panel. Changes take effect immediately and reset the calendar for a fresh run with the new parameters.

1
Call Duration Weights
Control what percentage of incoming calls are 1h, 2h, 3h, 4h, or 6h jobs. Default: 1% / 1% / 40% / 50% / 8%. Higher 6h weight stresses the Expert tech more.
2
Skill Level Weights
Set the probability mix for New / Specialist / Pro / Expert calls. Default: 40% / 57% / 0% / 3%. Increase Expert weight to create more scheduling pressure.
3
Hours Per Day & Call Count
Adjust the workday length (default 9h) and total calls generated per run (default 90). Longer days = more capacity. More calls = more overflow pressure.
4
Technician Roster
Add or remove technicians and set their skill levels. The calendar columns adjust automatically. Default roster has 1 Expert, 1 Pro, 6 Specialists, 1 New.
Applying settings clears the current schedule and resets all state. Make sure to save any data you need before clicking Apply.
